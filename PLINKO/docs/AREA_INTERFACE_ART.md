# Area interface art

4 November 2026 · CHG-046 / AF-010 · v0.7.11-preview/code 38 delivered

CHG-047 retains these native assets and adds the [Gelato alignment and upgrade-layout correction](UPGRADE_LAYOUT.md), delivered in v0.7.12-preview. The v0.7.11 art delivery below remains its original milestone.

Gelato Garden inherited eleven of Bakery's fourteen interface textures. Crystal Candyworks inherited all fourteen. Their environments and Settings/Pause frames were distinct, but buttons, HUD details, upgrade tiles and journey medallions still carried Bakery materials. The user requested separate area art and a slight change to the cream-wave transition.

Each area now has an explicit interface resource contract. Gelato and Crystal require all fourteen named roles in their own resource set. Missing or incomplete area metadata must fail the area's readiness check instead of silently copying Bakery textures. Bakery retains its existing individual PNGs. The gameplay, save, reward, purchase and control-layout rules are shared deliberately.

| Area | Interface materials | Native resource | Transition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bakery | Mint enamel, warm parchment and copper | Existing `data/art/ui/` individual PNGs | Existing neutral white `cream_wave.png` |
| Gelato Garden | Lavender and mint glazed ceramic, ivory faces and restrained copper | `data/art/gelato/ui/controls.png` and `regions.json` | `gelato_cream_wave.png`, mostly white with restrained Gelato accents |
| Crystal Candyworks | Pale aqua sugar glass, ivory faces and restrained peach/copper details | `data/art/crystal/ui/controls.png` and `regions.json` | `crystal_cream_wave.png`, mostly white with restrained Crystal accents |

The fourteen roles are `panel`, `button_mint`, `button_cream`, `timer`, `coin`, `node`, `upgrade_tile_green`, `upgrade_tile_red`, `upgrade_tile_cream`, `upgrade_parchment`, `level_badge_cream`, `level_badge_mint`, `level_badge_muted` and `level_selector`. The stable role names preserve existing call sites. Green, red and cream continue to identify affordable, unaffordable and owned upgrades. Live locks, checks, selection rims, labels, prices and counters remain separate from the generated artwork.

Atlas regions are explicit normalized bounds. The loader confines references to the appropriate area's art directory, validates finite in-bounds rectangles, and caches each native texture. Existing nine-slice rendering stretches quiet face/edge spans while keeping corners readable. The earlier Gelato partial overrides and three themed menu frames remain in provenance/history; they no longer define the complete control-set contract.

Gelato and Crystal panel slices include the full painted horizontal rim, so stretching the quiet face does not pull decoration across HUD, result or upgrade-card text. Crystal's Home selector painting extends 14 logical pixels above and below its existing rectangle to keep its crest clear of both live lines. Text positions and touch bounds remain unchanged. The final review uses `visual-final-02` and `ability-final` captures; earlier captures remain iteration history.

The wave remains a 0.62-second presentation. Each transition captures its area artwork when it begins and retains it through synchronous destination changes. Opaque runtime coverage still hides the outgoing page before the destination is revealed. Home Play keeps its tray morph, results keep the frozen-board modal, and Pause/Resume keep the sharp-menu fade over blur. Reduced Motion settles immediately. Artwork selection does not change transactions or player saves.
