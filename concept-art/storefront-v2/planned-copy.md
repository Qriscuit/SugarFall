# Proposed copy for later features

Concept only. These paragraphs are not part of the listing exports. Daily challenges and Mabel's continuing presence across areas are not current gameplay.

## Daily challenges

Take on a new challenge each day, try a different setup and earn coins for completing its goal. Beat an optional harder objective for a bigger reward to spend on your next upgrade.

## Mabel's continuing presence

Mabel returns as you discover new areas, with a welcome, a quick tip and a celebration when you clear a difficult tray. From your first doughnut to your latest upgrade, there's a familiar face along the way.
