## Core rules

- Break every peg before the timer expires. Retries are free and reset the board.
- A fresh run starts with 10 seconds. Shift Clock adds 0.5 seconds per level, up to 15 seconds before bonuses.
- Ordinary Bakery pegs have 6, 12, 18 or 24 HP across four material bands. Bronze starts at six hits from an unupgraded normal ball. Core upgrades reduce the hits needed; special balls have distinct effects. Armored pegs have additional HP.
- Damage earns a fixed coin budget per peg, released in proportion to actual HP removed. Overkill or dividing damage into more hits cannot increase that budget. Bakery and Gelato use six damage coins per ordinary peg and nine per armored peg; Crystal uses eight and twelve. Break rewards, productive bin catches and clears are separate income.
- Every catch adds 0.1x to that cup's multiplier immediately, with no progress dots or growth cap. A standard cup adds 1.1, then 1.2 credits internally on its first two catches. Gold cups start at 3x plus Gold Bins upgrades; seeds earn half the current multiplier. Cup growth resets with each run. Fractional credits accumulate to two decimal places through collection, purchases and saves, while coin labels show whole values rounded down. Multipliers still show their decimal growth.
- Bakery and Gelato offer one optional time-circle pickup per run. Hover, touch or drag onto it for up to two extra seconds. Clock pegs add 0.5 seconds; combined time bonuses are capped at three seconds. Mabel holds the first circle during the guide. Crystal uses the four-second ×2 Prism offer instead of time-circle pickups; clock pegs still extend its run.
- Drag to aim the automatic feeder. Special foods appear by chance when owned and equipped; there are no manual attacks or special-shot controls.
- Keep your earnings on a timeout, then upgrade and retry. All twelve boards remain free.

Whole-coin presentation is centralized in `scripts/coin_display.gd`; it does not change rewards, affordability or saved balances. Wallets and earned totals show the floor of their precise value. The upgrade panel always shows the full price; a failed purchase message rounds its shortfall upward, so a balance of 29.99 against a 30-coin upgrade still needs 1 coin. Catch popups show the actual increase in the visible earned total and omit `+0`; fractions continue accumulating even when a catch crosses no whole-coin boundary. The large result total and collection message show the visible wallet increase after adding the complete payout, including carried wallet fractions, earned bonuses and the existing 60-credit milestone conversion when applicable. The button simply says **Collect** and awards that amount without repeating the number. For example, an internal 12.80 wallet plus a 1.30 payout becomes 14.10: the wallet changes from 12 to 14 and the result shows 2.

The end-of-run summary uses an area-specific illustrated counter, a ceramic dish of reward coins, and a large live payout directly over the scene. A freestanding signed comparison shows **run earnings against the previous run in the same area**: green for more, red for less, neutral **0%** for equal earnings, **First run** without history and **Up from 0** when a percentage is undefined. It uses precise earned coins before test-ad/toolkit bonuses, milestone conversions or wallet rounding; the payout remains the actual credits to collect. A conditional **Base … · last …** line clarifies bonus, fractional or wallet-carry cases. Two unboxed, icon-led stats show Cleared and Best chain, each with its previous value when available. Collect remains the primary action at its existing position, with a quieter **Test bonus +N** action below it. The previous stack of earnings, comparison and stat cards is retired.

All three areas remember their latest two finished campaign runs independently across restarts. Clears, timeouts and early Finish & Collect results count; practice Trials do not. Reopening an uncollected result preserves its original comparison. Missing or malformed optional history leaves progression intact and starts a fresh comparison baseline; Reset progress clears the history. First, equal and zero-baseline results use concise truthful messages without inventing a percentage. Nonzero changes below 0.1% retain their direction. Where base earnings are shown, a positive value below one coin remains “<1” so it cannot be mistaken for zero; ordinary whole-coin wallet and payout displays stay unchanged. The result art contains no baked-in words or numbers; live game state supplies every label. See [the art specification](docs/ART_DIRECTION.md#results) and [result art prompts](concept-art/results-v1/prompts.json).

The monetization service is an explicitly labeled local simulation. No real advertisements, payments, accounts, analytics SDKs or application network calls exist. Historical balance measurements exclude paid benefits and rewarded bonuses.

## Test ads and purchases

From Home open the small menu, then **Settings → Ads & purchases · test**. These controls operate on the current campaign's local simulation; test purchases do not cost money. **Reset progress** also clears these simulated benefits, fake store ownership and ad-test state.

- **Automatic ads:** a new completed campaign result can open a simulated ad only after at least four completed attempts AND 120 seconds of active campaign play since the previous fullscreen ad. The first ad also requires 120 seconds. Clearing a board or running out of time counts; early Finish and Collect and practice trials do not. Menus, the Home-to-play entry, pause and background time do not advance the cooldown. Active time spent in an abandoned campaign attempt still counts toward the time gate.
- **Shared cooldown:** opening any available fullscreen test ad resets both counters, including a voluntary rewarded ad that is cancelled. Missing inventory skips the interruption immediately; there is no queued ad that appears later on the current result or after pressing Play. Results are saved before an automatic ad opens. Restoring an interrupted result never replays the ad.
- **Rewarded ads:** finish a campaign run, choose **Test ad · bonus**, then **Start test ad**. Completing the test grants +50% of that result's base credits, calculated internally to two decimal places. The displayed bonus shows the extra whole coins that collection will add. Cancel, unavailable or failure grants nothing. The bonus stays on the result until Collect and survives restart. A duplicate completion or collection cannot pay twice.
- **Test purchases:** Remove Automatic Ads suppresses automatic breaks while leaving optional rewarded ads available. Baker's Toolkit adds 20% of normal campaign run earnings when collected. This does not multiply the rewarded bonus or the separate milestone voucher-conversion credit. For example, 10.10 base credits + 5.05 rewarded bonus + 2.02 Toolkit bonus adds 17.17 internally; from an empty wallet, the visible collection is 17 and 0.17 remains stored. Trials remain unaffected.
- **Checkout outcomes:** Approve grants the permanent test benefit once. Pending grants nothing until approved; Cancel or Fail grants nothing. Unfinished simulated requests are discarded on restart, while completed purchases persist until a reset. Restore Test Purchases copies this campaign's fake store ownership back to local benefits.

**Test controls** shows the two cadence counters. **Stage next ad break** sets three attempts and 120 seconds so the next completed campaign attempt reaches both gates; Remove Automatic Ads still takes precedence. Toggle ads unavailable to test uninterrupted results. **Clear local benefits** keeps fake store ownership so restoration can be exercised. **Reset monetization test** clears counters and all simulated purchases; it does not undo credits already earned or reset game progression.

Implementation: `scripts/monetization_service.gd` holds the policy and fake request/response contract; `scripts/monetization_ui.gd` draws the test screens; `main.gd` connects campaign timing, result grants and profile persistence. Requests have unique IDs and only the current request can resolve. The optional `monetization_test` save field is independent of the strict interrupted-run schema, preserving older saves. Local ownership is test data, not production purchase verification. Connecting real providers will require SDK adapters, account/product configuration, consent handling and platform purchase verification/restoration.

The isolated regression runner includes `tests/monetization_qa.gd`. GPU previews use the real Windows renderer; neither screenshots nor these local tests establish Android billing, ad delivery or physical-device behavior.

## Resume and compatibility

Runs checkpoint every five active seconds, on pause/focus loss and orderly close. Home-to-play entry does not consume checkpoint time. Reopening restores the final gameplay board paused, with no offline timer advance or partial-entry replay. A sudden crash can lose progress since the latest successful checkpoint. Physical Android process-kill behavior is untested.

Existing wallet, upgrade levels, unlocks and completed pending rewards are preserved where valid. An unfinished run from an older balance fingerprint is discarded while workshop progression remains. Old Trial best times are reset because they are not comparable under the new rules. Reduced Motion remains available in Pause.

Interrupted runs must match the current content fingerprint or an explicitly reviewed compatibility pair. The metadata-only compatibility bridge introduced in v0.7.1 still accepts that exact preceding Bakery fingerprint because only tray teaching text changed; Gelato and Crystal fingerprints are unchanged. The earlier combat/reward revision still rejects its incompatible old active checkpoints safely while preserving campaign progress and completed pending rewards. Historical compatible v0.4/v0.5 runs used the following migration: Retired queued shots, forced specials, Pulse charge and module cooldowns are cleared; RNG state and earned progress are retained. Older impact balls migrate to the larger pumpkin collider with any already-used crush spent.

Legacy integer wallets and run earnings remain compatible with precise fractional catch payouts. Changing the visible coin format does not round stored balances or require a save migration. Historical evidence for the decimal-bucket update is in `../verification/decimal-buckets/` (388 gameplay/save/input assertions and 29 GPU preview checks); its decimal labels describe the older presentation.

The game uses one canonical save, `user://sugarfall_save.json`, and now writes profile version **5**. Area records from versions 3 and 4 migrate without resetting valid wallets, upgrades or progress; missing legacy Second Prism ownership becomes false. Gelato keeps its exact-node ownership. Bakery and Gelato run checkpoints remain schema 4; Crystal uses schema 5 for its run-local Prism opportunities. Earlier Main/Fresh Test files remain migration/recovery sources, not separate selectable campaigns. Updating the game alone never performs Reset progress.

The [v0.7.4 export manifest](docs/ANDROID_SUGARFALL_v0.7.4.json) verifies the same preview package and signing certificate as v0.7.3, with code increased from 28 to 29. The layout/flow update adds no save-schema or gameplay-fingerprint migration. Actual Android install-over-save testing remains open. Never uninstall an older build just to resolve a signature conflict without considering local save loss.
