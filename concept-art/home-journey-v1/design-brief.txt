HOME + LEVEL JOURNEY / concept pass / 23 October 2026

STATUS
Design proposals only. No runtime screens, saves, economy or area progression
changed in this pass. Illustrations are composition/material references, not
production sprites. Use separate assets and live content when implemented.

HOME
Top: a prominent clickable current-level plaque, for example Bakery · 4 / 12,
with a winding-path icon and chevron. The entire plaque opens the level map.
A small settings/menu control keeps Help and Trials accessible.
Wallet remains exact and readable. Center the current tray as a tangible food
object, without another surrounding content card. Its production preview must
use the actual selected level's peg arrangement and food assets; the concept's
eight cookies illustrate styling, not the final level-4 configuration.
Bottom: one dominant Play action and one quieter Upgrades action.
Remove the slogan, large game title, redundant level heading, instructions and
separate Trays button. Do not add an invented energy system, stars or offers.

LEVEL MAP
Open centered on the current level. One finger scrolls vertically through an
original winding bakery trail, from level1 at the bottom to level12 at the top.
Desktop wheel scrolls this map. Unlike the freeform upgrade map, this linear
journey needs no zoom controls or pinch interaction.
Show about five or six comfortable targets at once, with continuing path and
partial edge nodes implying scrolling. The page itself is the map; no inset box.
The phone concept shows levels2–7. The full-route illustration describes the
entire scrollable content and is not intended to fit inside one phone viewport.

Completed: ivory biscuit/ceramic, live number and check.
Current/selected: mint enamel, copper selection rim, small donut marker.
Locked: muted cream, live number and padlock; never purchasable or selectable.
Keep red reserved for unaffordable upgrades. Do not invent star ratings.
Use the existing 200ms tiny tap bounce and Reduced Motion behavior for available
level selection. Keep labels and state icons separate from generated art.

NAVIGATION
Home Play -> current selected level, one tap.
Home current-level plaque -> map centered on that level, one tap.
Map available node -> selection; fixed Play · N -> run directly.
Map back -> home. Completed levels in the active area remain replayable.
Future nodes remain locked. Clearing any replay must not skip progression.
Upgrades -> existing Recipe Trails; its Play action remains direct.

AREA HANDOFF
Clear level12 (not merely select or reach it), collect the result, then enter a
short area-completion/next-area step. The gateway and environment preview make
the next destination visible. Berry Orchard is an illustrative proposed theme,
not a finalized requirement. Other food environments can use the same grammar.
The next area's local ladder begins at level1 with local upgrades reset. The
proposed reset also clears local coins/equipment/unlock vouchers; finalize the
exact reset contract before implementing persistence or balance.
Communicate the actual changes briefly, e.g. new area name, Level 1,
Upgrades reset, Coins -> 0 (if coins reset), and Continue. These are functional
consequences, not flavor text. No surprise reset on an ordinary map tap.
Preserve prior area completion history, settings and permanent paid ownership,
including Remove Ads, Baker Toolkit, restore records and entitlement state.
Do not use the full-profile reset function to advance an area.

CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION BOUNDARY
The game currently has one area of 12 levels. Clearing 12 sets chapter_complete
and leaves current_level at 12. A new area ID, per-area progress, reset contract,
save migration and theme selection are future implementation work.

STYLE
Keep softly painted 2.5D food, rounded clean forms, cream ceramic, mint enamel,
fine copper fittings and upper-left light. Fraunces for short actions/area names,
Nunito for digits and operational labels. The proposed orchard changes scenery,
food accents and material colors while keeping controls recognizable.
Generated scenes are an art-direction target, with fine background detail
reduced during production if it competes with phone-size targets or labels.
Only useful labels: area/level, currency, Play, Upgrades, numbers and state marks.
No flavor copy, slogans, gesture hints or explanatory decoration in the UI.

FUTURE ASSET BREAKDOWN
Reuse: food sprites, coin, primary/secondary button skins, fonts, live check and
lock symbols, gameplay tray, cups and hopper.
Create: quiet home background; blank current-level selector if existing panel
cannot support its icon/chevron; modular vertical bakery map scenery; blank
round level medallions for completed/current/locked states; small donut marker;
area-end gateway; next-area environment pieces and appropriate food variations.
Render route geometry, labels, counters, selection and progress live in Godot.
Generate individual art pieces with real transparency where needed; do not
slice text-bearing concept screenshots into runtime controls.

NEXT DESIGN CHECK
Review home hierarchy, path readability and the proposed orchard transition.
After selecting the direction, create individual assets, integrate the screens,
then implement and validate area progression separately from art replacement.
