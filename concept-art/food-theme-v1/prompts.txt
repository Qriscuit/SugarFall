01-gameplay-kitchen

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high fidelity illustrated mobile game gameplay screen, a food-themed art direction for the existing Peg Foundry peg-and-ball game.
Primary request: Create one beautiful portrait 9:16 screen, edge to edge, no phone device or surrounding presentation board. The game drops donuts instead of balls. A pomegranate special splits into exactly three ruby-red seed fragments. A heavy pumpkin projectile is only about 25 percent larger than a donut. This is an appetizing miniature kitchen toy.
Style: premium art-directed 2.5D game illustration with sculpted volumes, hand-painted highlights, beautiful baked dough, glossy icing, ruby fruit and warm wood textures. Visually rich and tactile, crisp readable silhouettes at phone scale. Contemporary charming game art, no characters or faces on food, no logos or watermark.
Composition: straight-on playable flat physics board within a mint-enamel baking tray with rounded copper fittings, pale parchment interior, small warm kitchen details confined outside its edges. The screen is mostly the board, a quiet low-contrast backdrop behind gameplay. A little donut hopper at the top has a visible curved feed spout and aiming indicator. Sixteen biscuit-shaped peg targets in compact staggered rows occupy the upper and middle board, with generous spacing. Each peg is recognizably a round cookie stud, with subtle segmented icing or cracks to communicate durability. Show a handful of small pink-frosted donuts falling and bouncing; their holes must remain obvious. Donut diameter about half a peg diameter. One round pomegranate splitting into THREE larger jewel-like seeds can be a readable mid-board moment. A small pumpkin in flight has a squat round silhouette and small stem, 25 percent larger than a donut. Restrained motion trails. Five ceramic catch cups at the base have clear multipliers; the warm gold center cup has 3x while the other cups have 1x.
UI: small cream header with timer "00:08", coin icon and "128", "LEVEL 03", and large pause button. Dark cocoa type, clear quiet labels. At bottom under the playfield a large mint "PULSE" action and two chunky round equipped-special buttons with pomegranate and pumpkin portraits. Keep touch targets spacious. No long explanatory copy, no shop offers, no ads, no energy or lives.
Palette: warm cream, toasted biscuit, muted mint enamel, strawberry pink, pomegranate ruby and pumpkin orange. Light from upper left, gentle contact shadows, polished handmade materials. Food and targets must be sharply distinct from the pale backboard. Make the art feel substantially richer than simple geometric code-drawn circles while preserving an understandable playable layout.

02-upgrades-kitchen-workshop

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high fidelity illustrated portrait 9:16 mobile game upgrade-map screen for a food-themed Peg Foundry.
Primary request: A delightful pannable upgrade map that feels like exploring a miniature kitchen workshop. Full screen edge-to-edge, no phone frame, no collage. The MAP and its art occupy about 72 percent of the screen. The layout must be artfully asymmetric and spatial, with winding progression routes and recognizable culinary landmarks, never an evenly spaced eight-spoke wheel.
Style: richly art-directed 2.5D hand-painted miniature diorama, slightly overhead view, warm wooden counter with cream parchment inlays, mint enamel, ceramic, polished copper, delicious dough/fruit textures. Match a playful premium food game with donuts, pomegranate splitters, and slightly larger pumpkin impact balls. Crisp silhouettes, subtle painterly texture, soft shadows, colorful but calm.
Map structure: a small central mixing-bowl workbench is the starting landmark, offset toward lower middle. Several clear curved paths meander around asymmetrically arranged kitchen islands: a donut conveyor and flour hopper for FEED; a copper oven for POWER; a wobbly gelatin stand for BOUNCE; a citrus rebound contraption for RICOCHET; a cut pomegranate with exactly three large seeds for SPLIT; a pumpkin roasting block for IMPACT; a honey jar and brass collection cups for GOLD; a whimsical egg timer for TIME. Group landmarks naturally into baking, fruit, and utensils areas with generous empty counter space between. Some farther paths and future stations continue beyond the viewport edge to imply exploration.
Interactive nodes must be large round biscuit or ceramic medallions sitting ON their curved routes, with clear physical connections. Show roughly 18 to 22 nodes in this viewport, mixed sizes for milestone landmarks versus ordinary tiers. Routes are legible, do not tangle or cross ambiguously. Each upgrade track is a sequential route; shared starting hub but no invented mutually exclusive choices. Owned nodes have warm golden icing and a tiny check; available nodes are saturated food icons with a light rim; locked nodes retain recognizable low-saturation icons and small locks. One pomegranate split node near the lower-right map region is selected with a clean bright mint ring. Visual state is obvious without relying only on color.
UI: compact top bar with back arrow, title "UPGRADES", a coin icon with "240", and recenter icon. Very short track labels, no repeated tier paragraphs. Two unobtrusive zoom controls tucked at the map edge. Bottom stationary cream selection panel, clear pomegranate icon and title "POMEGRANATE"; one short line "Splits into 3 seeds"; a large mint button "60 coins". A separate large "PLAY" button anchored at the bottom. This UI is a believable usable mobile screen: readable type, generous button sizes, safe margins.
Important: this is a skill-upgrade map, not a linear numbered campaign map. Landmarks are decorative anchors for branching upgrade routes. Avoid evenly radiating straight spokes, rectangular dashboard cards, tiny illegible labels, huge character mascots, excessive decoration behind paths, social badges, ads and currency clutter.

03-upgrades-edible-garden

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: alternative high fidelity portrait 9:16 food-themed mobile game upgrade-map screen for Peg Foundry.
Primary request: Explore a visually distinct "edible garden" upgrade map: a miniature food landscape on a large serving platter, branching paths curling through islands of baked bread, fruit and kitchen gardens. Full-screen edge-to-edge mobile UI, no phone bezel, no collage. This should feel like an inviting place to pan around and discover upgrades, while the actual graph stays readable.
Style: beautifully painted 2.5D casual-game diorama, gently elevated top-down view, sculpted rounded shapes and expressive appetizing food textures. Warm toasted bread terrain, mint cream channels, pomegranate ruby, pumpkin orange, strawberry glaze and leafy green accents. Premium cohesive game art, little surface irregularities and soft shadows, clear silhouettes. No characters, no faces, no candy-game imitation or logos.
Spatial layout: the starting node is a tiny ceramic mixing bowl on a central toast-shaped island, off-center toward the bottom. Curving biscuit stepping-stone routes fan out irregularly and weave around landmarks at varied distances: a donut bakery on a bread hill (FEED), small glowing bread oven (POWER), springy jelly grove (BOUNCE), citrus slice garden (RICOCHET), pomegranate orchard with a cut fruit and three ruby seeds (SPLIT), pumpkin patch (IMPACT), honey terraces with collection cups (GOLD), and herb garden with an egg-timer tower (TIME). Baguette bridges and piped-icing routes connect areas, but all graph lines must be readable and no route crosses another ambiguously. Routes continue partially offscreen. Do not arrange the islands in a symmetric ring or radial wheel. Display about 20 chunky tappable nodes in the viewport, not every tier in the entire game. A few little crumbs and leaves give scale, never clutter.
Progression states: completed biscuit nodes have a check mark and golden edges, available nodes show saturated food-specific icons, locked ones show muted but recognizable food icons and small locks. Node art matches its upgrade station. Use a distinct mint ring around the selected pumpkin node. Keep branching upgrade routes, not a single numbered level road; all routes are sequential upgrades accessible from the common origin, no mutually exclusive or cross-branch prerequisites implied.
UI: crisp compact top bar "UPGRADES", back arrow, coin "240", recenter icon. Small plus and minus controls at map edge. The bottom fifth of the screen is a calm cream selection panel with a pumpkin portrait, title "PUMPKIN", short line "Crushes the first peg", large button "70 coins" and an ample "PLAY" button. Large legible cocoa typography, generous finger-sized targets, clear separation of foreground controls from map artwork.
This is a considered alternative to a kitchen-counter upgrade map: more fantastical, storybook and organic, with environmental landmarks communicating what each track does. Rich art and spatial layout take priority over explanatory text. No reward popups, ads, energy meters, timers, giant mascot, photorealism, rigid spokes or repetitive flat circles.
