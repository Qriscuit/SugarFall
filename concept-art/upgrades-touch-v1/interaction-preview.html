Interaction concept · 01

A recipe that grows with you.

Soft ceramic tiles, winding copper paths, and food you can almost pick up. The map moves under your fingers; your coins and purchase button stay put.

Try the interaction

Local design study. Illustrative progression and values. No real purchases, game launch, account connection, or saved progress.
1
Touch the map, not zoom buttons.

One finger pans. Spread two fingers to zoom in; pinch to zoom out. A mouse wheel zooms toward the pointer.

2
See what your coins can do.

Green tiles have a plus and price. Red tiles have a minus and price. Owned tiles are cream with a check. Selection gets its own copper outline.

3
One purchase. A small ripple.

Buy Gold to watch newly unaffordable tiles bounce. Earn coins to reverse it. Only changed tiles react; the camera stays still.

Expanded example: Feed 1 is owned. Select a tile, then buy from the fixed drawer. Try Gold first.
